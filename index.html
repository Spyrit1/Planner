    How to make a daily schedule in three steps

    1. Write your day

      List tasks the way you'd say them: "standup at 11, 30 min gym, report before noon".

    2. Build the schedule

      Daywright reads times, durations and deadlines, then time-blocks your focus work, meetings and personal tasks.

    3. Adjust as the day moves

      Drag blocks to new times, stretch or shorten them, and tick tasks off as you finish.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Daywright free to use?

    Yes. There is no sign-up and no payment needed.

    Does Daywright use AI?

    It uses a smart text parser that runs in your browser to understand times, durations and priorities in your sentences. It doesn't send your text anywhere.

    Is my data private?

    Your plan is saved only in your own browser storage and never sent to a server.

    How does it choose the order of tasks?

    Fixed times stay put, deadline tasks go first, focus work takes your sharpest hours, and exercise goes last so overruns can't push it out.
